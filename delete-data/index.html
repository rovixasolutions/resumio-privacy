About this page

This page explains how users of the Resumio Android app (developer: Rovixa Solutions) can delete data stored on their device and request deletion of any related data processed through Google services used by the app.

Resumio does not require an account. Resume content is stored on your device, not on Rovixa Solutions servers.

Steps to delete your data

Step 1 — Delete resumes inside the app (recommended first)
  1. Open Resumio on your Android device.
  2. On the Home screen, open the resume you want to remove.
  3. Tap the delete control and confirm.
  4. Repeat for every saved resume you want removed.
Step 2 — Remove all remaining app data on the device
  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Resumio.
  2. Tap Storage (wording may vary by device).
  3. Tap Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall Resumio.

Clearing data or uninstalling removes local resumes, preferences, exported PDF copies kept in the app folder, and local Premium unlock status on that device.

Step 3 — Request help from Rovixa Solutions (optional)

If you need confirmation, have questions, or want us to help with a deletion request related to Resumio, email:

Email a deletion request

Or write to rovixasolutions@gmail.com with subject line Resumio data deletion request.

We aim to respond within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Deleted by you on the device: resume / CV content, drafts, template choices, app preferences, and PDFs stored in the app’s local folder.
  • Deleted when you clear data or uninstall: all Resumio app storage on that device, including local Premium unlock flags.
  • Not held by Rovixa Solutions as a cloud account profile: we do not operate a Resumio user account database of your CV content.

What may be kept (and for how long)

  • Google Play purchase records: payment and subscription history is handled by Google Play. Rovixa Solutions does not store your card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Google AdMob / Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: these Google services may retain advertising identifiers, diagnostic, or aggregated usage data under Google’s Privacy Policy. Rovixa Solutions cannot manually erase Google’s independent retention stores; uninstalling and resetting your advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads) limits further association where applicable.
  • Email correspondence: if you contact us about deletion, we may keep the request email and our reply for up to 24 months for compliance / support records, then delete or anonymise it.
  • Android backup / device transfer: if your phone backs up app data to your Google account or another backup, residual copies may remain until you remove them from that backup service.
