Resumio Privacy Policy

CV & Resume Builder by Rovixa Solutions

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

Resumio (com.rovixasolutions.resumio) is a CV and resume builder app from Rovixa Solutions.

Contact: rovixasolutions@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No account is required to use Resumio.
  • Your resume content is stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal resume information.
  • Ads (free plan), in-app purchases, crash reports, and analytics use Google services as described below.

3. Information stored on your device

Resumio may store locally on your phone:

  • Resume / CV content you enter (name, contact details, summary, experience, education, skills)
  • Template choices and saved resume drafts
  • Premium / purchase unlock status
  • App preferences (for example language and rating prompt settings)
  • PDF files you export (saved in the app’s documents folder on your device)

This data stays on your device unless you share a PDF yourself, uninstall without keeping app data, or use Android backup / device transfer.

4. Advertising (free users)

Free users may see ads via Google AdMob (publisher pub-4201617832387911), including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads (for example to unlock free-template PDF downloads).

Google may process advertising identifiers, device and usage data, and approximate location according to Google’s Privacy Policy. Where required (for example EEA/UK), consent may be requested via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Premium removes ads and unlocks Pro templates without watching rewarded ads.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Google Play Billing. Resumio only receives purchase / entitlement status to unlock Premium features and restore purchases. Payment details are handled by Google, not by Rovixa Solutions.

6. Crash reports and analytics

Resumio uses:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability diagnostics
  • Firebase Analytics — aggregated app usage events (for example feature use and app health)

These services help us improve reliability and performance. They are not used to sell your resume content.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network — ads, purchases, analytics, and crash reporting

Resumio does not require login, contacts, or microphone access to build a CV.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Firebase) as independent processors / service providers under their policies when you use ads, buy Premium, or when analytics/crash tools run.

If you use Android’s share sheet to send a PDF, the destination app (email, WhatsApp, Drive, etc.) is under that app’s privacy policy.

9. Children

Resumio is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

10. Data retention and deletion

Local resume data remains until you delete it in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall Resumio.

For questions about privacy or deletion, email rovixasolutions@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Resumio after updates means you accept the revised policy.
